## Custody verdict

**A. What a fixed, correct external authority enforces.** Each head identifies its case and exact bytes. Its roster commits to the latest revision of **each** assertion, including withdrawn heads. Both assertion and receipt history sets must match and include the accepted predecessor's histories. The registry independently rejects duplicate or noncontiguous revisions and checks the head receipt's reviewer allowlist membership, binding to the evidence and assertion, current decision and validity interval. A revoked head invalidates active reliance on it. An applicable uncited adverse finding can still withhold the claim. Old observations and source catalogues do not have the same cross-head monotonic-history check as decisions. [Custody contract](../eval/attack_evidence_custody_v1.py#L23) · [Head checks](../eval/attack_evidence_custody_v1.py#L44) · [Revision rules](../eval/attack_fact_registry_v1.py#L197) · [Receipt rules](../eval/attack_fact_registry_v1.py#L349)

The **15 custody tests** passed, including adverse-head omission even with the current byte pin replaced, missing/wrong/skipped/cross-case predecessor, immediate rollback, omission of revocation/withdrawal/supersession, and a repinned successor erasing already accepted history. In addition I checked these controls in memory using the fixture's genuine test interfaces. Item count is the number of claims passed on for analyst review. **B. What requires authority control.** The two pre-adverse rows use identical evidence. Replacing the *whole* authority restores the old decision. The wrapper has no independent clock, assignment channel, remembered head or receipt issuer. A party controlling these inputs can present a self-consistent earlier world. Supplying evidence bytes, an earlier time in a proposal, or a different receipt string gives no such authority. A custodian who never records an adverse decision can also commit to an incomplete roster. Matching a digest proves completeness relative to the supplied roster only.

**C. What is missing operationally.** There is no demonstrated authenticated collection origin, independently held completeness ledger, persistent monotonic head service, trusted clock or verified human reviewer identity. The test issuer builds its head and rosters from its fixture. The separately pinned demo exercises the adapter directly. It does not exercise an independent custody service. Downstream consumers must use the per-claim review list. In the rerun `mixed` carried exactly one item. ## Gates for a feasibility study

| Owner | Required procedure and evidence for sign-off |
| --- | --- |
| Data owner and privacy officer | Sign a scope naming permissible event profiles, purpose, collection interval, users, storage location, retention and destruction date. Minimize fields, pseudonymize identities while preserving approved joins, separately hold the reidentification key, restrict access and rehearse deletion, withdrawal and incident response using synthetic records. |
| Source owner and independent custodian | Record authenticated acquisition, export window, coverage/gaps, native IDs, generation, clock bounds and source digests at collection. Log transfers, revisions and access. A separately authorized person verifies collector origins and relay dependencies against source-owner records. Unknown origin, coverage or session scope must remain explicit. |
| Custodian independent of coordinator | Issue authenticated case assignments, current heads and accepted predecessors through a separate channel. Maintain persistent atomic head transitions, reject stale authority after restart, and reconcile the active roster to a separately controlled intake ledger. Demonstrate detection of missing adverse records, withdrawals and revocations, including changes received after initial scoring. |
| Review authority and time authority | Verify qualifications, identity, authority period and conflicts of interest separately from the coordinator and evidence producer. Bind receipt issuance and revocation to identifiable signers, case, evidence revision and decision. Use an authenticated time source and defined cutoff/expiry semantics. Independently rehearse expiry, revocation, receipt key loss and rollback. |
| Two reviewers and a separate adjudicator | Independently assess the same deidentified original evidence, blinded to model/guard outputs and one another's decisions. Record per-event facts, positive and adverse statements, joint/alternative evidentiary support, uncertainty and linkage. Lock both submissions before adjudication. Retain disagreements, qualifications, independence records, revisions and adjudication rationale. |
| Research lead and stakeholder | Before intake, register a fresh cohort, unit of analysis, inclusion/exclusion rules, denominators, coverage floor, sample-size rationale and confidence bounds. Measure field agreement per adjudicated field, wrong candidate joins per proposed join, missed joins per adjudicated true join, false eligibility per adjudicated negative/ambiguous claim, unsupported review items per emitted item and complete eligible positives per adjudicated positive. Report abstentions, invalid cases, disagreements and incident-clustered uncertainty. Historical exposed fixtures are development material. |

**Stop criteria.** Quarantine the affected batch and pause intake or release at the first unsupported eligible claim, cross-case/tenant join, missing adverse decision, unverifiable current head or time, privacy breach, or loss of independent review. Preserve the original decision and reconcile any already issued review items. Resume only on documented custodian, data-owner and research sign-off. A feasibility run that emits zero items cannot demonstrate usefulness. Freeze the positive coverage floor and acceptable statistical uncertainty before seeing results. Production would require a separate prospective validation and authorization.

## Recommended owner decision

1. **Approve preparation only.** The coordinator can draft the protocol and responsibility assignments and rehearse them on synthetic inputs. Real-telemetry ingestion, reviewer enrollment and finding-level study execution remain closed.
2. **Authorize one bounded Sol Medium assignment if desired.** Add a separately versioned offline admission contract for explicit collector ancestry, typed uncertainty keys and affirmative process/session semantics. Require paired controls for each row above, including a disputed uncited sibling, an unaffected event, explicit independent roots, shared/unknown roots and exact per-claim outputs. Preserve v1 pins and historical guards. If changing byte-pin ordering, cover mismatched bytes before decoding and continued rejection of repinned history omissions. Require all 228 historical tests, pinned demo and all 71 hashes to remain reproducible. Return the failing-before/passing-after controls for independent review.
3. **Keep the two sign-offs separate.** The engineering reviewer signs the corrected offline contract. The data owner, independent custodian, review authority and study lead sign the operational gates. The owner can then make a distinct intake decision. This checkpoint implemented no correction and used only existing synthetic fixtures.
