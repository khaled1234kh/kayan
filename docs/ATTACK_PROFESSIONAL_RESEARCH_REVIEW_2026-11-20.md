# Professional review of KAYAN’s ATT&CK evidence mapping

**20 November 2026, Africa/Cairo.** AI cybersecurity research review of code, stored outputs, and 56 fictional incident cases. The work supports an exploratory comparison of local retrieval methods and documents substantive evidence-interpretation failures. Hybrid E5-192 remains the leading challenger. Field-weighted BM25 remains the reference. The available evidence supports neither a validated superiority claim nor a deployment decision.

## Review method and provenance

The analyst read both observation-only packets and checked ATT&CK mappings against the hash-verified local Enterprise 20.1 STIX bundle. The observation-first judgments were saved and hashed before opening the author fixture or model-output artifacts in this pass. Prior conversation context included examples and scores from the diagnostic work, so the review cannot be described as independently blinded. The structured review records observations, dispositions, minimal evidence groups, alternative techniques, uncertainty and confidence for every case, with active STIX object IDs, versions and parent relationships. Confidence describes the strength of each judgment under its fictional record. It is not an estimated probability. ## Taxonomy and original label quality

The review agrees with 30 diagnostic decisions and proposes marking two positive labels ambiguous. `fresh-01/E2` establishes that an outsider used an existing Active Directory domain account to perform an update. It leaves authorized delegation unresolved. Domain Accounts is plausible if “outsider” was intended to mean unauthorized rather than external. The alternative Create Account → Domain Account lacks an observed account creation. `fresh-05/E2` establishes an unapproved cloud role assignment, but the withdrawn sign-on alert leaves both the actor and account control unresolved. ATT&CK’s Additional Cloud Roles describes adversary activity on an adversary-controlled cloud account. Both original labels are compatible with a less conservative reading. Neither has been replaced. [All 32 author-label comparisons](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/label_disagreement_report.md)

Other positives have defensible distinctions. `fresh-09/E2` makes screen acquisition visible without showing an RDP login. `fresh-13/E2` and `fresh-15/E1` distinguish remote from locally stored mail. `fresh-17/E2` and `fresh-19/E1` distinguish a powered-down host from a stopped service on a running host. `fresh-21/E2` and `fresh-23/E1` distinguish deletion of existing events from disabling future capture. The scoped `fresh-27/E1` discovery judgment remains medium confidence because the incident framing identifies an affected host while the collection vantage is unstated. [Case table](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/case_review.md)

The provisional cases also exposed granularity choices. `confirm-05/E2` establishes impersonation of a trusted organization and targeted delivery. The record leaves attachment maliciousness and initial-access intent unproven, so the supported judgment is T1684.001 Impersonation. `confirm-17/E2` supports a targeted lure through an external service, with no successful login or known collection objective. `confirm-09/E2`, `confirm-21/E2`, and `confirm-23/E2` support parent techniques for interpreter execution, scheduling, and archiving respectively, with no verified implementation detail to select particular children. ## Dataset construct validity

- **Clear paired contrasts, narrow population.** Each diagnostic positive has a paired negative, the expected outcome alternates with case-ID parity in all 32 rows, and four unrelated filler sentences occur eight times each. The prompt sends the records and technique references, so the case ID, author answer and family are absent as explicit fields. A case-ID hash does order references. No direct parity leak was demonstrated. Obvious cues such as “intruder,” “approved,” and “no corroboration,” repeated structure, and verdict-like incident summaries can reward lexical recognition. The study has no prevalence or external-validity evidence for real SOC records. [Diagnostic packet and prompt implementation](../eval/reports/ai_retrieval_mapping_20261120/blinded_review_packet.json) · [Request construction](../eval/ai_mapping_ablation.py)
- **Joint evidence is scarcely exercised by these cases.** All 26 supported judgments have one sufficient source record at the benchmark’s record granularity. Some records summarize multiple underlying sources, but those sources are not individually selectable. In the 24-case cohort, E2 holds the sufficient record in 11 of 12 supported judgments. This supports a finding about record construction, not a measured model shortcut. Earlier unit tests validate compound evidence mechanically. Neither cohort establishes that a model can combine independently supplied sources. [Structured evidence groups](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/case_review.json) · [Mock tests](../tests/test_ai_mapping_confirmation.py)
- **Ambiguity can change the denominator.** An unconfirmed alert and documented benign behavior both require abstention within the study, but provide different tests of the model. Explicitly separate insufficient evidence, prevention, authorization, simulation and unresolved technique ambiguity. Freeze exclusions with a rationale before scoring. No current sensitivity statistic makes the labels human-validated. [Label comparison](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/label_disagreement_report.md)

## Retrieval and interpretation

The diagnostic arms were reasonably matched for the intended comparison. Both used the same pinned local model and corpus, temperature, output budget, five references, common schema and prompt, with request order rotating by case. This isolates the chosen retrieval strategies at the level of the supplied candidate sets, not the causal contribution of individual embedding dimensions or document fields. There was one execution of each arm per case in the matched run, and Ollama managed the context in that historical runner. The separately locked confirmation explicitly requests context size. [Historical manifest and outputs](../eval/reports/ai_mapping_ablation_20261120/manifest.json) · [Confirmation runner](../eval/ai_mapping_confirmation.py)

The 192-token cap cut off 567/706 E5 passages, versus 64 at 512. The strategy-name suffix was already past the cap in at least 549 passages at 192 and 60 at 512. No diagnostic query exceeded 48 tokens. The cap therefore changes which catalogue text can be embedded and systematically reduces suffix exposure. It does not prove systematic *mapping* degradation. E5-only top-five hits increased from 3/16 to 6/16 at 512, while hybrid top-five hits dropped from 14/16 to 12/16; BM25 alone was 12/16. Rank fusion changes several candidate positions at once. The generator independently clips candidate descriptions at 400 characters, shorter than 700/706 full descriptions. Section-aware text and candidate-rank analyses are hypotheses for a separate study. Median retrieval was about 1.88 ms for BM25 and 20.41 ms for hybrid-192 in the stored run; the corresponding estimated combined medians were 645 and 661 ms. Extracted latency and memory are host-specific. ## Scoring, reproducibility and engineering

1. **Exact case accuracy conceals missed positives.** BM25 was exact on 22/32, with 8/16 true positive cases and 14/16 negative abstentions. Hybrid was exact on 26/32, with 11/16 true positive cases and 15/16 negative abstentions. Both arms abstained 18 times. The interpretation-prompt arm abstained on all 16 negatives, recovered 7/16 positives and missed nine pairs. Pair F1 and false/missed pair counts need to be shown beside exactness and coverage. The frozen comparison treats a valid empty array as a legitimate abstention and a malformed response as an error. [Stored mapping summary](../eval/reports/ai_mapping_ablation_20261120/mapping.json)
2. **Pair scores weight equivalent evidence differently.** The implemented alternative rule gives one TP for the sufficient singleton E3 and two for the jointly sufficient E1 + E2. A partial citation E1 can produce 1 FP and 2 FN while abstention produces 0 FP and 1 FN because the missed reference group is selected by overlap. This is the documented policy and was reproduced against the real scorer. It intentionally penalizes incomplete claims. It also gives arms different effective denominators if they select valid alternatives of different size and can make abstention preferable to incomplete but relevant evidence. Report technique selection, complete groups, record-pair scores and citation-binding failures separately. For a future study, preregister per-technique group success and explicitly examine cost sensitivity. [Scorer](../eval/ai_confirmation_scoring.py) · [Mock verification](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/technical_review_checks.json)
3. **Verified implementation defect — label feasibility.** An otherwise valid mock adjudication with five techniques that each require two records passed the adjudication validator. The corresponding complete answer failed the eight-entry prediction schema. The validator checks active IDs and minimal evidence groups without checking whether the total minimum complete evidence fits the response contract. No supported AI judgment in these cohorts reached that limit. Future protocol versions should validate the summed minimum group sizes before freeze or version the output contract. Preserve the current lock. [Adjudication source](../eval/ai_confirmation_review.py) · [Prediction contract](../eval/ai_retrieval_mapping.py) · [Reproduction evidence](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/technical_review_checks.json)
4. **Verified semantic limitation — citation relevance within a record.** The official scorer accepted a mock quotation about an unrelated meeting in the correct gold record as exact because its content binds literally. Even a complete approved record group does not verify entailment by the quoted span. This is consistent with the documented binding-only design. Keep claim-level conclusions and quote sufficiency separate from schema success, and add evidence-span checks in a new protocol if they are part of the claim. [Scorer and technical evidence](../eval/ai_confirmation_scoring.py) · [Mock verification](../eval/reports/ai_professional_review_20261120/technical_review_checks.json)
5. **Current reproducibility is bounded.** The historical and repeated hybrid measurements used the same 32 logged contextual ID lists and prompt hashes. Their raw-response strings matched in 22 cases and case scores matched in 29. The changed scores were fresh-07, fresh-13 and fresh-19. This establishes response variation under the logged conditions, with hardware, server state or sampling details unisolated. A minor code gap also remains in the frozen confirmation runner: the post-run `any(...)` context check passes vacuously if the resident-model list is empty. The request still specifies context and model, but an empty residency result cannot verify actual loaded context or VRAM. No confirmation run was performed to test the service response. [Original and repeated response ledgers](../eval/reports/ai_retrieval_mapping_20261120/responses.jsonl) · [Repeated ledger](../eval/reports/ai_mapping_ablation_20261120/responses.jsonl) · [Confirmation runner](../eval/ai_mapping_confirmation.py)
6. **Security boundaries are separate from mapping accuracy.** The offline evaluation invokes a local classification model and cannot establish operational authorization, semantic truth, tenant isolation, or safety under active conditions. The project’s experimental status returned `enabled: false` and the selected session digest matched prior evidence. The source rejects non-mock SovereignEG transport and checks an independently authorized engagement for the separate analysis workflow. The human gate verifies input shape, digests, agreement, adjudication and attestation; the claimed identity and independence still depend on the coordinator. This review neither tested live pentesting nor exercised an authorization bypass. ## Statistical and publication judgment

The family-paired bootstrap is appropriate as a descriptive adjustment for related cases. Six cases differed in exactness between BM25 and hybrid, with five hybrid-only and one BM25-only; 21 were exact for both and five for neither. Exact-case gain was 12.5 points with a 95% family-bootstrap interval of 0 to 25. Positive-case recall varied by +18.75 points with an interval of −6.25 to +43.75. These intervals permit no improvement, and a bound near zero is not an equivalence test. The seven-arm design, small 16-family cohort, and post-inspection selection make it inappropriate to treat the interval as a confirmatory winner test. Retrospectively omitting both disputed positives as a sensitivity check on saved outputs gives 21/30 for BM25 versus 25/30 for hybrid. The same directional signal survives this local choice. This is a post-hoc subset with no new inference or change to historical results. [Contingencies and paired estimates](../eval/reports/ai_mapping_ablation_20261120/mapping.json)

Publication is defensible as a reproducible engineering and error-analysis case study if the fictional data, analyst exposure, selection, drift, mechanical label checks and human-validation gap are explicit. A stronger study needs evidence groups spread across independently described records, better-balanced evidence discriminators, outcomes evaluated blind to model output, and a new independent confirmation cohort. If qualified human reviewers are unavailable, a separately authorized AI-labeled exploratory study can examine mechanisms and reliability, with its status stated explicitly. No new experiment was executed in this review. [Research validity assessment](ATTACK_RESEARCH_VALIDITY_2026-11-20.md) · [Prioritized corrections](ATTACK_RESEARCH_CORRECTIVE_ACTIONS_2026-11-20.md)
