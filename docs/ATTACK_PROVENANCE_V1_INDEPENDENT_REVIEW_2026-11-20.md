# Independent security and research-readiness review of offline provenance v1

20 November 2026 · Decision for the project owner

**No-go for enrolling real telemetry in a finding-level feasibility study with v1 as implemented.** Three deterministic counterexamples admitted a finding despite known uncertainty, conflicting linkage or dependent attribution. Each occurred with valid synthetic review receipts. The defects matter even when analysts control intake because legitimate disagreements and catalogue inconsistencies are precisely what this layer is meant to handle. Protocol drafting, reviewer recruitment and synthetic remediation can proceed. Real-data intake, operational decisions and model experiments require separate decisions.

## Root of trust and revision review

**Integrity boundary.** Verification trusts the caller's expected full-manifest SHA-256, reviewer-to-approved-receipt-digests mapping and evaluation time. Source payloads are length/digest-checked against the pinned manifest. Statement locations resolve within a particular record revision. Active assertions require an approved, in-window head receipt whose digest matches the external allowlist and whose content binds evidence inputs and the exact assertion. The case ID and catalogue contents are included in the evidence snapshot. Receipt text, source name and an enrolled-looking reviewer name have no authority on their own. Suggestion records take a separate path, and extra suggestion fields in a trusted snapshot fail strict validation. The current tests exercised stale evidence, changed assertions, invalid pins, expiry, unenrolled reviewers, revoked head receipts, withdrawals and applicable uncited contradictions. [Verification](../eval/attack_fact_registry_v1.py#L261) · [Receipt tests](../tests/test_attack_fact_registry_v1.py#L76) · [Suggestions](../tests/test_attack_fact_registry_v1.py#L100) · [Adverse evidence tests](../tests/test_attack_fact_guard_adapter_v1.py#L88)

**Control required.** Replacing bytes under the old full pin fails. Fabricating new source evidence and new affirmative assertions ordinarily requires influencing the snapshot approver and obtaining matching allowed receipt digests, or compromising the caller that supplies both. A reviewer misinterpreting genuine source data can also admit a false assertion. The registry authenticates neither the collection endpoint nor a person's identity. The demo's pins, data and issuer are intentionally co-located. Anyone controlling the whole fixture environment can regenerate apparent approvals. There is no such issuer invocation in the registry verification function. The adapter selects the case from the verified manifest, without a separate expected-case argument; a study coordinator must bind the assigned case to the expected pin. The caller's clock controls expiry. Earlier snapshots and verification times can reproduce old decisions. Operational currency requires a separately administered head and clock. [Digest definitions](../eval/attack_fact_registry_v1.py#L180) · [Test issuer](../eval/fixtures/ai/attack_fact_registry_v1/demo.py#L130) · [Adapter entry](../eval/attack_fact_guard_adapter_v1.py#L159)

**Design limitation demonstrated by selective omission.** In memory, add a reviewed, applicable `authorized` decision alongside the existing `unauthorized` decision. The result is **none_eligible**, with conflicting evidence. Remove only the adverse assertion and keep its receipt. With the original full pin verification is **invalid**. With a replacement full pin and the **same original receipt allowlist** the result is **all_eligible**. Receipt snapshot binding excludes the assertion and receipt collections, while active receipt validation iterates only surviving assertion heads. Contiguous revision checks operate inside one supplied snapshot. They cannot authenticate continuity across snapshots. This is a dependency on the manifest approver's completeness, not a SHA-256 bypass. For study use, the independent custodian should sign or independently pin a case-specific active assertion roster, prior head digest, withdrawals, revocations and cutoff. Reject rollback, dropped adverse heads and orphan active approvals against that record. ## Normalization, projection and remaining limits

- **What held in the supported XML profile.** The code selects Security-channel 1102 v0 from Windows Eventlog and its namespaced LogFileCleared subject fields. For 4624 v2 it selects the target account and target logon ID from named EventData fields. The fixture includes distinct subject and target tokens. Required paths must resolve uniquely. Size is capped at 65,536 bytes and depth at 12. The first parser rejects DTD and external-entity declarations before tree construction. UTC conversion preserves up to nine fractional digits, original hex lexemes survive alongside integer values, and unsupported profile versions receive a diagnostic. These are bounded decoder properties, not a general EVTX or XML-security certification. [Parser](../eval/attack_windows_event_normalizer_v1.py#L25) · [Normalizer](../eval/attack_windows_event_normalizer_v1.py#L89) · [Fixture regressions](../tests/test_attack_windows_event_normalizer_v1.py#L37) · [Microsoft 1102](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-1102) · [Microsoft 4624](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/previous-versions/windows/it-pro/windows-10/security/threat-protection/auditing/event-4624)
- **Design limitation — correlation and projection are independent.** The candidate function compares caller-supplied tenant, stable host, authority, generation and uncertainty bounds. A known mismatch excludes a candidate. Missing generation or unknown clock error in an otherwise matching record can withhold a candidate. The adapter does not invoke that function or require a candidate for eligibility. It trusts independently reviewed associations. In the demo the candidate and verdict are computed separately. There is no automatic candidate-to-attribution transition. Equally, a candidate result cannot certify the reviewer's linkage. A study must distinguish independent manual linkage from an assertion claiming to depend on automatic linkage. For the latter, retain and validate the exact candidate decision, scope, source digests and reviewer rationale. [Candidate](../eval/attack_windows_event_normalizer_v1.py#L182) · [Demo](../eval/fixtures/ai/attack_fact_registry_v1/demo.py#L162)
- **Design limitation — one primary cite represents a reviewed bundle.** The first statement supplies the projected v4 citation. The reverse trace carries every supporting statement, receipt and event for each projected assertion at that address. In the Windows example the clear cite suffices for the mechanic at guard level and the companion logon appears in its trace. This can be appropriate for analyst retrieval. It overstates model evidence completeness if scored as though the submitter supplied both citations. The trace groups by event/address and may return several associations rather than identifying a minimal decisive witness. Rejected claims carry reasons without reverse traces of excluded or adverse proofs. Record submitted citations, required joint witnesses, credited assertion IDs and decisive contrary evidence separately. Until then, require analysts to inspect the linked records for every proposed release. [Projection and reverse trace](../eval/attack_fact_guard_adapter_v1.py#L42) · [Observed companion evidence](../tests/test_attack_fact_guard_adapter_v1.py#L20)
- **What held at the verdict boundary.** The adapter checks the union and disjointness of eligible/rejected technique IDs, eligible case and citation agreement, and reverse provenance. With a valid v4 result it returns one entry per submitted technique. Duplicate pairs invalidate the envelope, empty valid proposals abstain and an unresolved sibling can remain withheld alongside one eligible review item. The flat format merges repeated occurrences of one technique. Neither batch state nor analyst eligibility constitutes permission to execute an action. [Result partition](../eval/attack_fact_guard_adapter_v1.py#L120) · [V4](../eval/attack_evidence_guard_v4.py#L11) · [Partition tests](../tests/test_attack_fact_guard_adapter_v1.py#L120)
- **Unverified operational risks.** No genuine enrollment, collector custody, production clock synchronization, calibrated timestamp uncertainty, de-identification effectiveness, operational data completeness or external validity was demonstrated. I found no demonstrated path for XML bytes or suggestions alone to impersonate a reviewer under separately protected correct pins. The review was targeted source inspection and deterministic testing, not an exhaustive security proof.

## Human-reviewed feasibility study gates

**Before intake.** Obtain written authority from the data owner for secondary research, an institutional privacy/ethics determination and applicable data-use terms. Document purpose, permitted fields, collection scope, retention, deletion, incident handling and who may access raw identifiers. Start with the minimum saved 1102/4624 records, generation/clock evidence and independently sourced custody/authority evidence needed for the question. Pseudonymize consistently within an approved identity namespace; preserve different tenants, alias relationships, intervals and collision tests. Keep original and transformed digests and the controlled transformation record. Keep re-identification keys and originals with the custodian. Only the approved offline study environment may access the study copy. If lawful basis, minimum fields or origin cannot be verified, exclude that case or suspend intake.

**Source and review authority.** A custodian independent of analysis must attest origin, collection/export method and generation, chain of custody, gaps, host/tenant mapping, calibrated time bounds and upstream collector genealogy. Pin an authenticated case roster and snapshot before review. Independently enroll at least two qualified endpoint/incident reviewers through verified identities and role records. Record conflicts and prohibit either reviewer from authoring the case or receiving expected labels, the other's findings or model outputs before submission. Give each immutable blinded source packages. Retain each original submission, evidence roles, candidate links, disagreements, qualifications and timestamps. A third qualified adjudicator resolves differences with cited reasons, including an explicit unresolved option. Lock adjudication and decision costs before inspecting prototype scores. The previously exposed cases remain development material. Never issue real receipts with the synthetic helper.

**Measurement plan.** Register an incident-family-separated set of fresh authorized cases before scoring. Include completed, benign, failed and ambiguous activity, aliased sources, contested identity and missing coverage. Record counts and denominators for every stage. Measure exact normalized fields per adjudicated field, unsupported or unresolved fields by profile, correct/incorrect candidate links per proposed link, missed links per adjudicated link, and appropriate abstentions separately. Measure **false eligible** per adjudicated negative/ambiguous claim and **unsupported review items** per emitted item. Also measure complete eligible positives per adjudicated positive, omissions and reviewer agreement. Evaluate joint statement sufficiency, not just technique IDs. Report paired counts by incident, review time and uncertainty at the incident level. A small pilot can establish feasibility even when it cannot estimate a rare error rate precisely.

**Decision gates.** Before study authorization require zero false eligibility on the three P0 counterexamples, their positive controls and sibling isolation; every missing or conflicting critical identity must resolve to invalid/withheld; retain the existing historical suite and verify the old artifact hashes. Before scoring session accuracy require the two candidate defects to pass their regression controls. Verify externally held pins, authenticated case assignment, trusted evaluation time, source lineage and adverse-head completeness through a dry run on synthetic data. Freeze denominators, positive coverage floor, intended false-release risk budget and sample-size rationale with the stakeholder. Do not import a case while these gates are open. During the pilot pause verdict emission on the first adjudicated unsupported eligible finding, stale or missing adverse receipt, unaccounted cross-tenant join, data-use breach or unverifiable source; preserve each failed case for analysis. A run with zero eligible findings cannot meet a positive coverage floor. Only after a separate, larger prospective validation meets prespecified upper confidence limits for false releases and lower limits for positive coverage, with independent external data, privacy review, operator review, rollback and shadow monitoring, consider a new live-integration decision.
