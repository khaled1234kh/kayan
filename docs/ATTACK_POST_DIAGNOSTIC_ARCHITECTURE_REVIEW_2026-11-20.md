# KAYAN TrustGate | Decision after ATT&CK Experiments A–D

20 November 2026 · Architectural review for the project owner and academic supervisor · Offline retrospective incident analysis

## Decision for review

**Prioritize proof of one identified event before optimizing its ATT&CK label.** Approve a narrowly scoped implementation proposal for a versioned offline evidence guard and its regression fixtures. The first milestone is that both independently supportable findings in `slate` can reach an analyst together, while borrowing evidence from another event still withholds a finding. This milestone requires no model. Preserve BM25 @ 5 as the retrieval baseline and E5-192 hybrid @ 5 as a research comparator. Defer Experiment E.

The larger architecture depends on a source of trustworthy event facts. Start with reviewed facts and deterministic normalization of fields whose semantics are documented. Model-proposed interpretations can be useful as analyst suggestions. Their promotion into the proof path requires separate evidence. A research finding passing the guard would establish eligibility for analyst review within this study’s completed-adversarial-activity definition. ATT&CK labels themselves confer no permission to act.

## Proposed architecture and trust boundaries

| Step | Input, output and authority |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Preserve original observations | Retain case namespace, immutable source/version, stable statement address, originating collector, raw value and time basis where available. A hash binds bytes. An authenticated collector identifies an origin. Neither establishes the meaning of a narrative allegation. |
| 2. Build event-scoped facts | For documented schemas, parse typed action, tenant, asset, principal/session, outcome and time with versioned rules. Unknown values stay unknown. Each join records its source keys, time interval and method. Human-reviewed interpretations use the same provenance contract with review identity and revision. Model suggestions stay in a separate unverified collection. |
| 3. Propose ATT&CK hypotheses | Query the pinned catalogue, retain candidate ranks and definitions, and propose a technique for an identified event. A candidate definition supplies taxonomy meaning. Original observations supply evidence. Candidate scores describe retrieval relevance. |
| 4. Check a claim against reviewed facts | Resolve one occurrence against an independently supplied registry. Require a witnessed action and objective, completion, attribution and authority for this study, plus any necessary process/session links. Evaluate adverse and superseding evidence even if the proposal omitted it. A proposal may point to an event but cannot create a trusted binding. |
| 5. Present a per-claim disposition | Show the event, technique, original supporting addresses by role, outstanding gaps and contrary records. Distinguish eligible for analyst review, withheld, ambiguous and invalid. Record revisions and analyst decisions. Evidence about historic authorization has no connection to permission to execute an operation. |

The “graph” initially needs only two small relations: **facts about an occurrence** and **source statements supporting each fact**. Each relationship needs provenance. One statement can legitimately support several occurrences through separately established relationships. No graph database, graph inference service or new agent is needed to represent this. Deterministic code can validate address resolution, schema types, explicit identity keys, interval overlap for an approved join rule, and completeness against a specified contract. A timestamp match alone cannot identify an actor. Narrative interpretation, source independence, disputed identity, ambiguous authority and semantic sufficiency require trusted normalization specifications or independent human adjudication. A verifier cannot manufacture those inputs.

Keep this design in offline evaluation. In the inspected source the research API reports disabled and rejects assessment/reindex requests with 503, the registry rejects SovereignEG, and its adapter requires a mock transport. No serving status was polled in this review. Nothing in the proposed path issues actions, grants authority, or feeds the live five-agent workflow. ## Smallest defensible guard correction

Current `review` groups pointers by technique, expands every pointer through an address-only index, then requires one event across the union. In slate, E1.1 independently binds B19 to both archive encryption and the snapshot purge. T1486 has its own action at E2.1, T1490 at E4.1 and its recovery outcome at E3.1. The frozen diagnostic admits each reviewed chain separately. Combining the fact banks rejects **both** with `source_or_event_mismatch`. This is a false withholding on authored normalized facts. The guard was outside the D scorer. [Guard](../eval/attack_evidence_guard_v3.py) · [Fixture definitions](../eval/attack_experiment_d_guard_v3.py) · [Retained outcomes](../eval/reports/attack_diagnostics_d_v3_20261120/offline-guard-diagnostic.json)

1. In a separately versioned offline guard, anchor the claim to exactly one independently reviewed, cited action matching the technique’s mechanic and objective. Missing or competing action anchors withhold. An uncited best-fit action cannot rescue the proposal.
2. Index trusted associations by case, event and source address. A shared cite may contribute only its explicitly reviewed association to the anchored event. Require every offered address to supply a relevant role there. Reject wrong-only citations and incompatible or competing action anchors. Never drop an inconvenient cite and then approve the remainder.
3. Preserve the full event-wide search for applicable adverse facts and conflicting process/session links, including uncited material. Require independent attribution, authority, completion and any event-specific links. Return event identity and reasons per claim. An empty proposal is an abstention. An eligible sibling cannot elevate a rejected claim.
4. For the bounded correction retain the flat proposal adapter. Withhold when it merges multiple occurrences of the same technique. A later versioned claim envelope can carry distinct event references and role citations, with each occurrence independently re-resolved. Keep the historical test that expects the old behavior. [Existing regression contract](../tests/test_attack_experiment_d_v3.py) · [Guard tests](../tests/test_attack_evidence_guard_v3.py)

**Additional limitations demonstrable in code.** The event tuple has tenant, asset and an already-correlated occurrence. There is no principal identity or timestamp interval in the fact type. The “time” regression changes the occurrence key, so it tests key isolation rather than raw timestamp interpretation. Independent attribution is represented by inequality of source strings. Aliases of one underlying collector cannot be detected from those strings. The guard resolves addresses against the supplied fact bank and has no original-record input. The D fixture separately checks address existence. Grouping by technique merges repeated occurrences, and the v2 output permits at most eight pairs. These are limits of what the interface can establish. They are not observed rates of false release. Preserve the existing checks while introducing explicit source lineage and reviewed join metadata where the data warrants them.

Two historical tests clarify what was demonstrated. The archived flint fixture already lacks a verified action, so its withholding alone cannot isolate retraction handling. A separate guard regression adds an uncited retraction to an otherwise eligible chain. Likewise, the D materialization tests contain both an unsupported actor string that passes address validation and hand-written compact overlays that fit all four templates. Those tests demonstrate separate properties of the interface. Neither establishes that an automatic extractor produces correct, compact facts. ## Validation and readiness

**Extraction evaluation.** Label assertions and joins separately, including explicit contradictions, unknowns, authorized intervals and whether absence reflects a collection gap. For each extracted item judge whether its cited text supports the asserted value **with its qualifiers**, whether the entity/event reference is correct, and whether a required fact was omitted. Report correct/supported assertions over all assertions, recovered required facts over labelled facts, and correct joins over proposed joins. Break out fabricated attribution, authority and completion, wrong time/tenant joins, fallback and abstention. Preserve adjudicated alternatives. Count critical unsupported assertions that pass the verifier separately from suggestions rejected before review.

**Next comparative study, conditional on approval.** Two qualified reviewers label fresh case families independently, blinded to outputs, retrieval arm and each other. Record qualifications, provenance, agreement by disposition and role, disagreements, adjudication and immutable hashes. Separate development from confirmation by incident/source family. Include real, de-identified and lawfully available telemetry with missing sources, duplicated collectors, benign lookalikes and contested outcomes. Keep the original 24-case human-gated protocol sealed. It has prior AI exposure. [Existing review workflow](../eval/ai_confirmation_review.py) · [Current handoff](../MASTER_HANDOFF.md)

Use direct mapping, matched mapping instructions with an empty overlay, and matched instructions with an unverified extraction as separate conditions. A reviewed-facts arm is a privileged upper-bound probe. A same-budget multi-call control can separate extra computation from passing information. Hold original evidence, candidate order and final response reserve fixed. Publish intended-assignment results with every invalid/fallback counted, then report delivery and conditional results separately. Report positive complete-group recall, ID-only selection, false findings per negative or ambiguous case, unsupported citations, evidence-pair scores, abstention, resource totals and uncertainty clustered by incident family. Publish outcomes from all registered passes. The critical contrast is a difference in **grounded findings**, alongside the cost of false accusations.

## Rejected or deferred

- **Experiment E** can probe candidate contamination. It cannot establish whether a selected technique has a complete, attributable event. Defer it until that question has a trustworthy measurement.
- **Automatic promotion of D2 facts or address-count heuristics** would accept semantically wrong but addressable statements. Keep unknowns, full context and reviewer-controlled promotion.
- **A graph database, autonomous verification agents or free-form graph traversal** add components without resolving who attested a relationship. Begin with versioned records and explicit joins.
- **Immediate E5 promotion, oracle substitutions in deployment, cloud inference and fine-tuning** lack confirmatory evidence for complete-group recovery. Additional model calls cannot repair an unknowable identity or a missing source. Maintain the existing provider restrictions.
- **Editing the v3 guard in place, changing frozen labels, pooling selected exposures as new cases or applying the research verdict to live execution** would undermine comparison or exceed the evidence.
